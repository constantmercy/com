Constant Mercy & Praise Children Home

Meet Our Founder

"Every child deserves to be loved, sheltered and given a chance to fulfill their God-given destiny."

Constant Mercy and Praise Children Home was founded by a woman of faith with a burning compassion for orphaned and vulnerable children. Moved by the plight of countless children living on the streets of Port Harcourt without shelter, food or love, she took a bold step of faith and opened our doors to welcome them home.

Her life's journey and unwavering belief that God cares deeply for the fatherless became the cornerstone upon which this ministry was built. Today, her vision continues to touch lives, transform destinies, and give hope to children who had none.

Under her leadership, Constant Mercy and Praise Children Home has grown from a small shelter to a thriving family where children receive holistic care — body, mind and spirit — and are raised to become productive citizens.

Our Story

How a heart of compassion became a home full of love.

Constant Mercy and Praise Children Home was born out of a deep burden for the orphaned, abandoned and destitute children on the streets of Port Harcourt, Rivers State. What began as a personal calling to rescue one child soon grew into a household of many — all loved equally, all valued immensely.

Located at No. 7 Road B, Washington DC, County Garden Estate, behind the NNPC Fuel Station, Opposite Timber Market, SARS Road, Rumuagholu, our home sits in the heart of the community it serves. Our doors are always open to children who have no one to call their own.

Over the years, the home has sheltered dozens of children — from infants to teenagers — providing them with warm meals, clean clothing, access to education, healthcare, and most importantly, a family. We celebrate their birthdays, cheer them through examinations, and pray together as a household of faith.

Every smile on the face of our children is a testament to the mercy of God and the generosity of the many supporters, donors and volunteers who believe in what we do. Together, we are rewriting stories — turning trauma into triumph, despair into destiny.

We continue to trust God for more resources to expand our capacity, renovate our facilities, and reach more children who need a place to call home. If you feel led to be a part of this journey, we warmly invite you to partner with us.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the story of Constant Mercy and Praise Children Home.

The Beginning

A Vision Born from Compassion

Our founder, moved by the sight of orphaned children on the streets of Port Harcourt, answered God's call to open a shelter and welcome the first children into her home.

Growing Years

Expanding the Family

Word spread about the home's work. More children were welcomed. Donations from well-wishers enabled the expansion of facilities, providing more beds, food and learning resources.

Education Focus

Investing in Education

We partnered with local schools to ensure every child in our care received formal education. Seeing our children graduate from primary and secondary school became our greatest joy.

Community Impact

A Pillar in the Community

Constant Mercy and Praise Children Home became recognized across Rivers State as a trusted, faith-based institution dedicated to child welfare and community transformation.

Today & Beyond

Continuing the Mission

We continue to grow, serve and trust God for greater capacity to reach more children. With your support, our best years are still ahead.

Mission, Vision & Goals

The compass that guides Constant Mercy and Praise Children Home.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, loving and Christ-centred home for orphaned and vulnerable children, equipping them with education, healthcare, spiritual grounding and life skills to become productive members of society.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where no child is left homeless, unloved or without access to education — where every orphan has a family and every life is valued as precious.

Our Goals

To shelter 100+ children, expand our educational support programs, renovate our facilities and reach more communities with the message of hope and restoration.
