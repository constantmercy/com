Welcome to Constant Mercy & Praise Children Home

A sanctuary of love, hope and restoration for children who need it most.

Nestled in the heart of Port Harcourt, Rivers State, our home is more than a shelter — it is a family. Every child who walks through our doors is embraced with unconditional love, given quality education, nutritious meals, healthcare, and raised to know their worth.

Founded on the principles of mercy, faith, and service, we believe that every orphan and vulnerable child deserves a safe, nurturing environment to grow, dream and thrive.

  • Safe, comfortable residential care for children
  • Quality primary and secondary education support
  • Healthcare and regular medical check-ups
  • Spiritual nurturing and character development
  • Life skills training and vocational guidance

Our Pillars of Service

Everything we do is guided by compassion, faith, and an unwavering commitment to the children in our care.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, loving and Christian home for orphaned and vulnerable children, equipping them with the tools to live meaningful, productive lives.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where no child grows up without shelter, love, education or hope — where every orphan knows they are valued, cherished and have a bright future.
